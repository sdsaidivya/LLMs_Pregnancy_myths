**Old Wives’ Tales Through Machine Eyes: Probing LLM Cultural Imagination Across 36 Countries**

It is an empirical study conducted to analyze cultural alignment and socio-economic sensitivity in Large Language Models (LLMs) over the pregnancy myths across 36 countries. 

The framework leverages a manually curated dataset of pregnancy myths alongside targeted country-unconditioned(baseline) and country-conditioned prompt sets over two scenarios to evaluate three LLMs behavior across clinical rationalism, cultural shielding, and stance alignment. 

These responses are used to compute social pressure, Reasoning ratio and MASI and at the later stage the numerical scores where correlated with the socio-economic factors.
